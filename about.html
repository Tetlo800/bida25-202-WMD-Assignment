Our Story

Founded in 2015, Cargo Worldwide Express started as a small courier service in Gaborone. Over the past decade, we've grown into a full-service logistics provider operating across 6 countries in Southern Africa and beyond. Our roots in the local market give us unique insight into the region's logistics challenges.

Why Us

We offer reliable tracking, competitive rates, and 24/7 customer support. Our platform integrates seamlessly with your business workflows, giving you visibility and control from first-mile pickup to last-mile delivery.

Mission

To simplify global logistics with technology and a human touch. We believe that world-class freight solutions should be accessible to businesses of every size — from the market stall to the multinational corporation.

The Team

Our team is made up of experienced logistics professionals and tech innovators who share a passion for solving complex supply chain problems. Together, we bring decades of expertise to every shipment.
